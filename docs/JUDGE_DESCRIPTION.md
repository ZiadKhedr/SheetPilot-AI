# SheetPilot AI

**Your Spreadsheets. Your AI Workforce.**

SheetPilot AI connects unfamiliar business Excel workbooks to a reviewed operational mirror and a Meta-powered team of logical agents. It reuses a read-only structural analyzer to preserve source coordinates, merged titles, hidden sheets and formula expressions, then asks Muse Spark to propose business mappings from bounded summaries.

Humans confirm mappings and cross-file relationships supported by actual identifier overlap. Dashboards calculate revenue, sales performance and replenishment needs from reviewed data. The Orchestrator calls typed Sales, Inventory and Procurement tools; calculations and money remain deterministic.

A proposal is not an execution. The Approval Center validates source versions, records the human decision and generates a real additional purchase-order workbook. Rejection produces no workbook, repeated approval is idempotent, and the activity timeline records actual events.

The submission includes two fictional companies with different English/Arabic schemas, automated backend/frontend/browser validation, and a verified official Meta API path. The MVP runs locally with FastAPI, SQLite and React. It does not claim live OneDrive/SharePoint sync or perfect arbitrary-file comprehension.
