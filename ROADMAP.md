## Next after the hackathon

1. Expand the labeled benchmark with authorized copies of real company files, including multirow headers, irregular regions and ambiguous schemas.
2. Make review/reconciliation richer: multiple semantic alternatives, reviewed cleaning proposals and stable record identities under row movement.
3. Add production identity/roles, tenant isolation, immutable audits and worker resource isolation.
4. Migrate the mirror to PostgreSQL/object storage when multi-user workloads justify it.
5. Implement OneDrive/SharePoint version-aware change detection, conflict review and narrowly scoped writes with rollback/reconciliation.
6. Extend controlled Sales/Inventory/Procurement actions only after sync and policy enforcement are proven.

Do not advertise any future phase as implemented. Approval remains the human boundary for consequential actions.
